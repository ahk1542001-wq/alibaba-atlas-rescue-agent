      How Atlas Journey works
      
                            
      
                                
      What is the Atlas Sandbox?
      
                                
      Flight offers come from the official Atlas Sandbox, not a runtime mock fallback. Sandbox results are not production airline inventory, and provider booking limits are shown honestly.
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      What does “approval” mean?
      
                                
      The agent never books on its own. Before a booking attempt you see the exact provider offer and price, then approve it yourself. Approval can still fail safely when Sandbox ticketing is unavailable.
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      How do I change my plan?
      
                                
      Every finished step shows an Edit button — press it to re-open that step. Steps after it are updated afterwards, and we tell you before anything changes.
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      What if my flight is disrupted?
      
                                
      If something changes after booking, your original plan stays visible. We show replacement options with reasons, and nothing is rebooked without a separate approval from you.
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      What do you store about me?
      
                                
      Only what you save in “Your details”, and only with your consent. Passport number, payment details, and legal identity are never collected or stored. You can delete any field at any time.
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Good goals include from, to and when
      
                                
      Example: “Bangkok to Singapore, Sep 29–30, plan my whole trip.” If we can’t work the trip out, we’ll ask one short question at a time.
